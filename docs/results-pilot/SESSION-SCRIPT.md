# Results-comprehension session script

Draft facilitation material for the [focused protocol](PROTOCOL.md), prepared 26 October 2026. The English result interface is the object of this test. Vietnamese prompts below are unreviewed author drafts for facilitation, not a translated assessment or evidence of language equivalence.

Facilitators: complete the protocol's operational fields first. Use the language the reader can comfortably discuss in and record the language used for each task. Keep language changes visible in the record. Do not treat an English explanation followed by a Vietnamese explanation from the same reader as two independent comprehension observations.

The italicized facilitator notes and “look for” guidance below are not participant prompts. Keep them out of the first unassisted reading. A correct-looking reply after you explained the intended meaning is not evidence that the original wording was understood unaided.

## Opening — about 3 minutes

*Use the operator's completed consent procedure. State the actual notes/recording, storage, retention, and withdrawal arrangements; do not substitute this generic introduction for those details. Check that the reader wishes to continue.*

**EN:** “We are checking whether this result screen is understandable and useful. We are testing the material, not your political knowledge. You can disagree with an explanation, skip a task, take a break, or stop. You do not need to tell me your political identity or defend a political choice.”

**VI:** “Chúng tôi muốn xem trang kết quả này có dễ hiểu và hữu ích hay không. Chúng tôi đang kiểm tra tài liệu, không kiểm tra kiến thức chính trị của bạn. Bạn có thể không đồng ý với phần giải thích, bỏ qua một nhiệm vụ, nghỉ hoặc dừng lại. Bạn không cần cho biết mình thuộc khuynh hướng chính trị nào hay bảo vệ một lựa chọn chính trị.”

**EN, synthetic route:** “These are made-up test answers. They are not your answers and do not describe you. Please read the first result as you would encounter it on your own.”

**VI, synthetic route:** “Đây là các câu trả lời giả định dùng để thử nghiệm. Chúng không phải câu trả lời của bạn và không mô tả bạn. Hãy đọc kết quả đầu tiên như khi bạn tự mở trang này.”

*For the own-quiz route, complete the ordinary assessment before any synthetic result or author material reveals a country correspondence. Do not give guidance about which political answers to select. Say that exporting or sharing their responses is not required for this session unless a distinct, agreed procedure explicitly covers it.*

## T01 — Explain a finding in your own words — about 5 minutes

*Use RP01, or the completed own-quiz result when that route was agreed.*

**EN:** “Take a moment to read this result. In your own words, what is it saying?”

**VI:** “Bạn hãy dành một chút thời gian đọc kết quả này. Theo cách hiểu của bạn, kết quả đang nói điều gì?”

**Neutral follow-up, EN:** “Which words led you to that understanding? What, if anything, is still unclear?”

**VI:** “Những từ hoặc câu nào khiến bạn hiểu như vậy? Có điều gì vẫn chưa rõ không?”

*Record the first explanation before asking the following boundary probe. Do not introduce labels such as “leftist,” “liberal,” “centrist,” or “hypocritical” as candidate answers.*

**EN:** “What would you feel comfortable concluding from this result? What would you want to know before making a broader conclusion?”

**VI:** “Bạn thấy mình có thể rút ra kết luận nào từ kết quả này? Bạn muốn biết thêm điều gì trước khi đưa ra kết luận rộng hơn?”

*Look for a reading of the expressed commitments and choices, rather than an attributed identity, hidden motive, or claim of scientific certainty. Record any criticism that the interpretation itself goes beyond its evidence, even when the reader understood it accurately.*

## T02 — Find and assess the supporting answers — about 5 minutes

*Use RP01. Keep the same finding open for the reading and evidence task.*

**EN:** “How would you check what this explanation is based on? Please show me.”

**VI:** “Bạn sẽ kiểm tra phần giải thích này dựa trên điều gì bằng cách nào? Bạn hãy thử cho tôi xem.”

*Allow an unassisted attempt. Do not point to the numbered links initially. If help is needed, record that before giving it.*

**EN, after navigation:** “What do these answers contribute to the explanation? Does the explanation fit the evidence you found? Tell me about anything that does not fit.”

**VI:** “Những câu trả lời này đóng góp gì cho phần giải thích? Theo bạn, phần giải thích có phù hợp với thông tin vừa tìm thấy không? Nếu có điểm chưa phù hợp, hãy cho tôi biết.”

*Use a finding connecting two commitments, such as public guarantees with private independence. The task is to understand the connection and evaluate its support, not to endorse those commitments. Record whether the link expanded and focused the intended answer, whether the reader noticed it, and whether returning to the interpretation was straightforward. A static booklet can test comprehension, but not the site's interaction; mark navigation as not tested in that mode.*

## T03 — Separate role action and personal preference — about 5 minutes

*Use the explicit role/preference difference in RP01. Inspect a situation without a selected personal package within the same case for the missing-preference contrast.*

**EN:** “What are the two choices being compared here? What is the difference between the questions that produced them?”

**VI:** “Ở đây đang so sánh hai lựa chọn nào? Hai câu hỏi dẫn đến những lựa chọn đó khác nhau ở điểm nào?”

**EN:** “What does the difference let the report say? Is there anything about the person's reason that the report cannot tell us?”

**VI:** “Sự khác nhau đó cho phép báo cáo nói điều gì? Có điều gì về lý do của người trả lời mà báo cáo chưa thể cho biết không?”

**EN, missing-preference contrast:** “In this other example, no personal package was selected. How would you read that case?”

**VI:** “Trong ví dụ khác này, người trả lời không chọn phương án nào cho sở thích cá nhân. Bạn sẽ hiểu trường hợp này như thế nào?”

*Look for action within the assigned responsibility versus optional preference among the same feasible packages and constraints. Missing, uncertain, and unrepresented preferences do not supply a concrete package for comparison. Do not suggest that a difference reveals a more authentic ideology or an actual causal effect of holding office.*

## T04 — Compare mixed and neutral results — about 3 minutes

*Use RP02 and RP03 in the recorded order. Do not explain the category names before the first comparison. The connected-belief case is RP01; do not substitute it silently for this contrast.*

**EN:** “What is different about the information available in these two results? What would you be able to say about each one?”

**VI:** “Thông tin có trong hai kết quả này khác nhau như thế nào? Với mỗi kết quả, bạn thấy có thể nói được điều gì?”

**EN:** “How would you explain a theme with answers pointing in different directions? How is that different from someone choosing ‘neither agree nor disagree’ throughout?”

**VI:** “Bạn sẽ giải thích thế nào khi các câu trả lời trong một chủ đề nghiêng theo những hướng khác nhau? Điều đó khác gì với việc luôn chọn ‘không đồng ý cũng không phản đối’?”

*Look for distinct commitments in the mixed record and explicit neutrality in the neutral record. Agreement with public ownership and competitive provision can concern ownership and operation separately; a cooperative package is a specific arrangement, not proof of a centrist identity. Preserve the reader's explanation before supplying those distinctions.*

## T05 — Read uncertainty, limited evidence, and an unrepresented choice — about 4 minutes

*Use RP04 and RP05. RP04 has explicit uncertainty; RP05 has one directional statement, skipped items, and “none of the offered choices” in the public-records case. Do not explain those facts as the intended interpretation before the reader examines the examples.*

**EN:** “What information is present in each result, and what is missing? How would you describe the difference between these cases?”

**VI:** “Mỗi kết quả có thông tin gì, và còn thiếu thông tin gì? Bạn sẽ mô tả sự khác nhau giữa các trường hợp này như thế nào?”

**EN:** “Only one statement in this example has a directional answer. What does that allow the explanation to say, and what remains open?”

**VI:** “Trong ví dụ này, chỉ có một nhận định được trả lời theo hướng đồng ý hoặc phản đối. Điều đó cho phép phần giải thích nói gì, và điều gì vẫn còn để ngỏ?”

**EN:** “What does ‘none of the offered choices’ tell you here? Is it the same as a neutral statement answer, uncertainty, or a skipped item?”

**VI:** “Ở đây, câu trả lời ‘không có lựa chọn nào phù hợp’ cho bạn biết điều gì? Nó có giống với một câu trả lời trung lập, sự chưa chắc chắn, hay việc bỏ qua câu hỏi không?”

*Look for uncertainty as unresolved judgment; skipping as no substantive answer; a single directional answer as limited evidence; and an unrepresented choice as rejection of the offered packages without identifying a preferred alternative. None supplies a political identity in place of evidence. If a booklet is used, it can test comprehension but not interactive navigation.*

## T06 — Read the housing comparison — about 3 minutes

*Use RP06; its selected reasons are part of the displayed record.*

**EN:** “What changed between the two housing situations? What does this report tell you about the two choices?”

**VI:** “Điều gì thay đổi giữa hai tình huống nhà ở? Báo cáo cho bạn biết gì về hai lựa chọn?”

**EN:** “What can we tell about why the answers stayed the same or changed? What additional information would help?”

**VI:** “Chúng ta biết được gì về lý do các câu trả lời giữ nguyên hoặc thay đổi? Thông tin bổ sung nào sẽ giúp hiểu rõ hơn?”

*Look for the repair deadline as the changed stated condition, while the action packages and other constraints remain fixed. The report can describe the observed choices and selected reasons. Fixed order, repeated presentation, and possible practice or consistency effects prevent attributing a change to the deadline alone. If one action is missing, there is no two-action comparison. Record the initial interpretation before offering this explanation.*

## T07 — Recognize what has not been tested — about 3 minutes

*Return to the knowledge or care/ecology section in RP01.*

**EN:** “Look at the knowledge theme or the care and ecology theme. What does this section give us information about? What would need another question or situation?”

**VI:** “Hãy xem chủ đề về tri thức hoặc chủ đề về chăm sóc và sinh thái. Phần này cho chúng ta biết điều gì? Điều gì cần thêm câu hỏi hoặc tình huống khác?”

**EN:** “If someone accepted more than one of these starting assumptions, how would you understand that?”

**VI:** “Nếu một người đồng ý với nhiều giả định nền tảng trong phần này, bạn sẽ hiểu điều đó như thế nào?”

*Look for premise endorsement as distinct from demonstrated decision behavior. The current quiz has no dedicated knowledge-conflict, care-allocation, or ecological-standing scenario. Multiple knowledge sources can be relevant without specifying which wins a conflict. Care and ecological dependence are separate claims, not one scale.*

## T08 — Country reveal, report controls, and close — about 6 minutes

*Use RP01, or an already completed own-quiz result. Demonstrate file controls with synthetic answers where possible.*

*The author workspace does not create a personal assessment save. For the erase probe below, refer to a hypothetical saved ordinary quiz or the agreed own-quiz route. Mark a verbal explanation as a conceptual observation, not a successful test of an erase control that was not used. The same distinction applies if a JSON or print control is unavailable in the chosen test mode.*

*Only use this task after a completed result has been reached. Do not ask for a real-country guess during an unfinished quiz.*

**EN:** “What does the setting's revealed real-world inspiration tell you? What does it leave uncertain about the situation or the person answering?”

**VI:** “Việc công bố quốc gia ngoài đời đã gợi cảm hứng cho bối cảnh cho bạn biết điều gì? Điều gì vẫn chưa thể kết luận về tình huống hoặc người trả lời?”

**EN, own-quiz route:** “When did you first notice or suspect a real-world correspondence? Did that affect how you read anything?”

**VI:** “Bạn bắt đầu nhận ra hoặc đoán bối cảnh liên hệ với một quốc gia ngoài đời từ lúc nào? Điều đó có ảnh hưởng đến cách bạn hiểu phần nào không?”

*Look for a fictional institutional setting with an inspiration, not a simulation of a country's people, a national stereotype, or evidence of the reader's nationality. Prior inference is possible; withholding an explicit label is not proof of blinding. If a synthetic example disclosed the mapping before the own-quiz route, record the exposure and do not count that session as a first-use concealment check.*

### File controls and closing prompts

**EN:** “If you wanted to keep a readable copy of this result, what would you use? What would you expect the file to contain, and where would it go?”

**VI:** “Nếu muốn giữ một bản kết quả dễ đọc, bạn sẽ dùng chức năng nào? Bạn nghĩ tệp đó sẽ chứa gì và được lưu ở đâu?”

**EN:** “What do you think happens when you erase the answers in this browser? What happens to a file that was already downloaded?”

**VI:** “Theo bạn, điều gì xảy ra khi xóa câu trả lời trong trình duyệt này? Một tệp đã tải xuống trước đó sẽ như thế nào?”

**EN:** “Which explanation was most helpful? Which was least convincing or hardest to understand? What would you change?”

**VI:** “Phần giải thích nào hữu ích nhất? Phần nào khó hiểu hoặc ít thuyết phục nhất? Bạn muốn thay đổi điều gì?”

*Use synthetic data when demonstrating downloads. A local file is not a research submission. Erasing this browser's answers does not erase exported files, separate reviewer notes, or copies in other tabs. Apply the operator's agreed deletion and retention procedure; do not collect a response export merely to prove a control worked. Confirm whether the reader wants any optional test save removed and close under the agreed procedure.*

## After the participant leaves

Separate unassisted paraphrases, neutral-probe responses, facilitator explanations, and later suggestions. Assign issue severity to the material or procedure, not to the participant. Keep substantive disagreement separate from misunderstanding. Record tasks that were not tested, sources of assistance, language switches, early exposure to country correspondences, and changes made during the session.

Summarize proposed revisions after the wave, with exact target IDs and wording. A revised finding needs a later documented check; do not fill the log with expected answers or present the synthetic examples as observed participant findings.
