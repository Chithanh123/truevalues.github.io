### Descriptive response comparison when repair urgency changes

Describe whether the selected housing package differs between the urgent and planned repair conditions while their shared facts and options remain fixed.

<details>
<summary>Evidence required and interpretation limits</summary>

**Evidence required:** Both housing-urgent and housing-planned responses in the recorded fixed order, their version and common scenario facts. Missing or non-choice responses remain distinct.

**Limits:** The repeated case contributes no additional aggregate score. A within-person difference is not an individual causal urgency estimate, a test of ideological consistency or a pure role effect.

Catalog outcome: `COMPARE-HOUSING-URGENCY`.

</details>

Record individual judgments about dispersed information, tacit knowledge, situated experience and revisability of explanations.

<details>

**Limits:** The four statements are not an expertise-versus-experience scale. Agreement does not establish research literacy, epistemic virtue, a preferred coordination system or a single political identity.
